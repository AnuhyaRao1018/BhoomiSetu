6. SLOT BOOKING ENGINE – VERY IMPORTANT
This is the most important feature.

Create a complete slot booking system.

Booking flow:

STEP 1: Select procurement centre.

STEP 2: Select crop type.

STEP 3: Enter estimated quantity in kilograms or quintals.

STEP 4: Select procurement date.

STEP 5: Display all available time slots.

STEP 6: Select one available slot.

STEP 7: Confirm booking.

STEP 8: Generate a unique booking ID and queue token.

Example:

Farmer: Ramesh Kumar
Centre: Warangal Procurement Centre
Crop: Paddy
Date: 15 November 2026
Time: 10:00 AM – 10:30 AM
Token: BS-WGL-001
Booking ID: BHOOMI-2026-0001

IMPORTANT SLOT VALIDATION RULES:
- Every time slot must have a predefined maximum capacity.
- A slot must never exceed its capacity.
- Once the slot reaches its maximum capacity, mark it as FULL.
- If a farmer tries to book a completely full slot, immediately display:
"Sorry! This slot is already booked and no more bookings are available. Please select another time slot."
- Never allow duplicate bookings for the same farmer, centre and date where they conflict.
- Prevent two simultaneous users from taking the final available booking position.
- Recheck availability on the server immediately before confirming a booking.
- Update available capacity immediately after successful booking.
- If a booking is cancelled, release its capacity.
- Do not count cancelled bookings as active bookings.
- Use database transactions or equivalent concurrency-safe logic.
Slot statuses:
- AVAILABLE
- FILLING FAST
- FULL
- CLOSED
Show capacity visually, for example:

10:00 AM – 10:30 AM
8 / 10 slots booked
2 slots remaining
[Book Now]

When all 10 slots are booked:

10:00 AM – 10:30 AM
10 / 10 slots booked
FULL
[Unavailable]

Show a clear disclaimer whenever the farmer selects a full slot.

One very important thing before building
Tell Antigravity to use Supabase with PostgreSQL, not just HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

Why? Imagine 10 farmers book the 10:00 AM slot. The 11th farmer must immediately see that the slot is full—even if they are using another phone. That requires a shared database and server-side booking validation.

Also, I recommend building in this order:
1. Authentication and database.
2. Farmer registration and booking.
3. Slot capacity and duplicate prevention.
4. Staff dashboard and queue management.
5. Notifications and analytics.
6. Final visual polish and deployment.
